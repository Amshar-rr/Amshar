# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want B4Thinkin' to be a friendly, respectful place for students, contributors and visitors of every background and ability.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and respectful to others
- Welcome questions, especially from beginners
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully
- Respect different opinions and experiences
- Focus on what helps students learn

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, bullying, insults or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or jokes
- Sexualised language or imagery
- Spam, trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior
- Sharing others' private information without permission

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behavior they consider inappropriate.

## Reporting

To report a problem, contact the maintainer (Amshar) through the contact details on the GitHub profile, or open a private security advisory if the matter is sensitive. All reports will be handled fairly and confidentially.
